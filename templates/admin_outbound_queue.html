Completed queue retention

Remove old sent, failed, and cancelled entries, including their history and queued copy. Active or uncertain deliveries are retained.

0 disables automatic cleanup.
When enabled, cleanup runs hourly in batches of up to 500 entries.

Archived bounce-address retention

Archived VERP mappings attribute late delivery bounces after their queue entries are removed. Keep them indefinitely (0, the default), or remove them after a chosen number of days. A bounce arriving after its mapping is removed will no longer be recognized.

0 keeps mappings indefinitely.
When enabled, cleanup runs hourly in batches of up to 500 mappings. Active queue references are retained.
